Greyed while playing together: Save, Load, Eject, Library, the debugger, and in Settings the choices that would change the game (such as what is plugged into the controller ports). Pause, Reset (and the choices under its arrow), zoom, filter, sound and fullscreen work. Pause and Reset act on both games.
