Playing a Super Scope game with the mouse — agreed

Desktop and web. Every Super Scope game connects the scope to port 2 automatically (unless the player chose a controller for port 2 in their settings). Port 1 stays a controller.

Super Scope connected — click to aim with the mouse

1. The game has just loaded. The message fades after a few seconds; the sight appears once the player clicks the game and the mouse is captured.

Super ScopeWhat the player presses
AimMove the mouse
FireLeft click
CursorRight click (two-finger click on a trackpad)
PauseThe Start key, 5
Turbo switchThe Select key, 4, toggles it; starts off each time a game loads
Turbo on

2. Playing: white ring sight with a black outline follows the mouse. Pressing 4 flips Turbo and says “Turbo on” / “Turbo off”.

Mouse released — click the game to aim again

3. After Escape: the sight is hidden and the normal pointer returns. The next click on the game captures the mouse again and does not fire.

Super Scope connected — click to aim with the mouse

4. Narrow web window: the sight scales with the picture and the message wraps inside it.
