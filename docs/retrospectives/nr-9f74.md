# nr-9f74 — closed as delivered when only its mockup had merged

## What happened
At 00:26 on 2026-10-24 the fleet view closed nr-9f74 as delivered, but the only thing on main was
the UX stage's mockup commit, `docs(nr-9f74): mockup (#3366)`. The build's PR, #3380, was still
open and had picked up a merge conflict with main. The producer's worktree held five uncommitted
lines that answered two of the cold-read findings. Cerebro reopened the bead; the next producer
committed those lines, rebased, answered the findings and merged.

## Why
Not established. A likely cause is that the delivery check matches any commit on main whose
subject carries `(nr-9f74):`, and the mockup commit carries the bead id as well.

## Cost
A producer pass to recover: reading the earlier pass's state, committing what it had left, a
rebase over a conflict in `web/src/app.ts`, and a second full CI run. The open PR also sat behind
main for most of a day.

## Prevent by
Before closing a bead as delivered, the fleet view's check should also require that the bead's
PR is merged, or that a `feat|fix(<id>):` commit is on main. A `docs(<id>): mockup` commit should
not count as delivery.
